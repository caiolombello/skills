# Register and examples

Use these to calibrate the amount of formality, not as templates to reuse. The facts in each brief are supplied for that example; do not borrow them for another message. Several registers can be equally good.

## Personal reply

Brief: A friend asks whether tomorrow at 19h still works. It does. No change of location was supplied.

Too much ceremony:

> Olá! Espero que esteja bem. Gostaria de confirmar que nosso encontro de amanhã, às 19h, permanece agendado. Será um prazer compartilhar esse momento!

Natural:

> Tá combinado, amanhã às 19h.

This degree of informality suits a friend. It is not a default for a client or an unfamiliar colleague. Do not add a venue or a claim that the speaker is excited.

## Workplace message with a tentative commitment

Brief: The colleague asks if the review will be done today. The speaker can try, but cannot promise.

Too polished and too strong:

> Pode deixar! Vou priorizar essa demanda e garantir a entrega da revisão ainda hoje.

Natural:

> Vou tentar revisar hoje, mas ainda não consigo te garantir.

Shortening must preserve the uncertainty. "Te aviso quando terminar" would be an additional commitment, so include it only if intended.

## Group notice with moderate formality

Brief: A team's meeting moved to Thursday at 15h, in the same room. Ask anyone who cannot attend to notify the speaker by tomorrow. Do not invent a reason for the change.

Too ceremonial:

> Prezados, venho por meio deste informar que, visando otimizar nosso alinhamento, a reunião foi reagendada. Contamos com a colaboração de todos para garantir uma experiência produtiva.

Natural:

> Pessoal, a reunião passou para quinta, às 15h, na mesma sala. Quem não puder participar, por favor me avise até amanhã.

The salutation can be omitted in an ongoing conversation. Avoid using "Pessoal" at the start of every group message.

## Email that keeps a boundary

Brief: A client named Ana requests a Tuesday delivery. The agreed deadline is Friday and Tuesday cannot be met. No alternative offer or apology was supplied.

Too soft:

> Vamos fazer o possível para atender à sua solicitação com a máxima agilidade.

Natural:

> Olá, Ana.
>
> Não conseguimos entregar até terça. O prazo combinado continua sendo sexta.

If a name is not supplied, do not invent it. A polite email need not evade a refusal or add concessions.

## Assistant explanation

Brief: Explain why the failed job will be retried. The supplied configuration allows three retries at intervals of five minutes.

Formulaic:

> Ótima pergunta! Não se trata apenas de executar uma tarefa, mas de garantir uma operação robusta e resiliente. Vamos explorar os principais pontos dessa jornada.

Natural:

> A configuração permite três novas tentativas, com intervalo de cinco minutos. Por isso o job será executado de novo após essa falha.

Do not infer idempotence, eventual success, or a retry policy beyond the configuration provided.

## Punctuation and protected text

In newly drafted prose, prefer "Recebi o arquivo. Vou conferir hoje." to "Recebi o arquivo — vou conferir hoje." The same applies to "Recebi o arquivo - vou conferir hoje."

Keep `segunda-feira`, `e-mail`, `--dry-run`, `https://example.com/meu-post`, negative numbers, and identifiers intact. For an existing direct quotation, keep its punctuation. If every dash character is forbidden in prose, rephrase rather than damaging spelling. Only replace "na segunda-feira" with a date if that date is known.

## No personal sample

Brief: "Write a polite follow-up to a supplier about the delivery date." No prior correspondence, name, deadline, or personal style sample is available.

Natural:

> Olá, você consegue confirmar a previsão de entrega?

No need to block on a sample, claim to reproduce the user's voice, or invent that a deadline has already passed. If the missing date is necessary for the requested message, ask for it; otherwise a neutral request is enough.
